# Training RQ: user guide

Open **https://hrforms.wct.my/WCT-Training-RQ/** and sign in with your WCT Microsoft account.

## Everyone: raising a request

1. Click **New Training Requisition**.
2. **Section 1** is filled in for you. If you're asked for your department, choose it once and it's saved.
3. **Section 2:** choose *Planned* or *Ad hoc*, then enter:
   - the title, **Objective** and **Relevancy** (how the training relates to the participants' work)
   - the **training dates** and time
   - the fee per participant, venue and provider
4. **Section 3: proposed candidates.** Choose one way to list participants:
   - **Type names (up to 8):** type each person's full name. If you're booking for yourself, type your own name.
   - **Upload name list (more than 8):** attach an Excel or CSV list and enter the **Number of participants**. The total fee uses this number.
5. **Supporting documents:** attach the brochure or quotation (PDF, Word, Excel or image, up to 5 MB each).
6. Click **Review & submit**, check the summary, then click **Submit to HOD**.

You can **Save draft** at any time. Your request then shows a reference number (e.g. `TRQ-2026-0007`), and you can follow it on the progress tracker.

**Approval route:** Department HOD → your BU Admin (Land, E&C or Malls) → L&D Team. For **Land and Malls** the L&D Team gives the final approval. For **E&C** the request then goes to the **HR Head** for final approval. The "Approval route" panel on the form shows the exact route for your business unit.

**If it's returned to you,** you'll get an email. Open the request, read the remark, click **Amend & resubmit**, make your changes and resubmit. It starts again at your HOD, and earlier versions are kept under **Versions**.

**Other actions:**

- **Clone** a past request to reuse its details.
- **Cancel** a request you no longer need. A reason is required.
- **Print PDF** gives you the official form layout. **Export to Excel** gives the same details as a spreadsheet.

## Approvers (HOD, BU Admin, L&D, HR Head)

- You'll get an email with an **Open request** button. **Approve or reject only inside the app.**
- **Approvals** in the sidebar lists everything waiting for you, oldest first.
- **Approve:** add an optional remark and confirm. The next approver is notified automatically.
- **Reject:** a remark is required. The request always goes back to the requester, who amends and resubmits; approval then starts again at the Department HOD. Everyone who already approved in that round is copied on the email.
- **L&D:** while reviewing, fill in **HRD Corp claimable** and the **grant no.** under *L&D assessment*.
- If someone else acted first, you'll see "already actioned by …" and the page refreshes.

## Business Unit Admins (Land, E&C, Malls)

You approve or reject requests from your business unit after the Department HOD has approved them. Any one BU Admin of the unit can decide. You can view all requests in your BU under **Training RQ History**, and the dashboard's **Analytics** section for your BU.

## HR / L&D: history and analytics

- **Training RQ History:** search by ref no., title, requester, provider or **candidate name**. Searching a staff name shows that employee's training history. Filter by status, BU, department, type, HRD Corp and dates, then click **Export to Excel**.
- **Overview & analytics** (top of the dashboard, in one bar with your own counters): requests and approved spend by month, business unit and department; planned vs ad hoc; and the average time requests spend at each approval step. Choose the year and export to Excel.

## L&D Team and System Admins

The L&D Team manages every training requisition, so L&D members have full System Admin access. The System Admin role stays for IT support.

**Admin settings** lets you manage:

- **Approval flow** (opens first): each business unit's route as numbered steps (Raise → Department HOD → BU Admin → L&D Team → HR Head → Approved).
  - Use the switches to skip the **BU Admin** or **HR Head** step for a unit. You'll be asked to confirm before a step is switched off.
  - Changes apply to the next approval. Requests already waiting at a step stay there and can still be approved.
  - **Edit descriptions** changes the short text under each step.
- Business units and BU Admins
- Head of Departments (each department and its HOD)
- Roles (L&D Team (System Admin), HR Head, System Admin)
- Users:
  - Every enabled WCT staff member is copied from Microsoft 365 each night, so you can assign anyone before they first sign in. **Sync now** updates the list straight away.
  - Use **Show** to list who hasn't signed in yet, or who is no longer in Microsoft 365. Nobody is switched off automatically; deactivate leavers here.
  - Fix unmatched departments here.
- Notification log, with **Retry** for failed sends

To add someone as a BU Admin, HOD or role holder, type two or more letters of their name or email in the picker, choose them from the list (or use the arrow keys and Enter), then click **Add**.

On a desktop, **Collapse sidebar** at the foot of the menu shrinks it to icons. Hover an icon to see its name. Your choice is remembered on that browser.
- **View as demo user** (your user menu): see the system exactly as a demo Requester, HOD, BU Admin, L&D or HR Head. A banner shows who you're viewing as; click **Exit** to return. Only `@demo.wct.my` accounts can be viewed.

Settings changes are still recorded in the database for security, but there is no audit log screen.

## Demo accounts (stakeholder trial)

While the trial is on, the sign-in page lists demo accounts under **Try a demo account**, grouped by role. Pick one to see that role's screens. A banner shows which demo account you're using; **Switch account** takes you back to pick another. Demo accounts only see demo requests, and Admin settings are read-only for them.

Changes apply immediately. For example, a newly assigned HOD sees pending requests from their department straight away.
