# Training RQ: user guide

Open **https://hrforms.wct.my/WCT-Training-RQ/** and sign in with your WCT Microsoft account.

## Everyone: raising a request

1. Click **New training request**.
2. **Section 1** is filled in for you. If you're asked for your department, choose it once and it's saved.
3. **Section 2:** choose *Planned* or *Ad hoc*, then enter the title, objective, dates, time, fee per participant, venue and provider.
4. **Section 3:** search for colleagues by name or email, up to 8. If someone isn't found, use *Add manually*.
5. **Supporting documents:** attach the brochure or quotation (PDF, Word, Excel or image, up to 10 MB each).
6. Click **Review & submit**, check the summary, then click **Submit to HOD**.

You can **Save draft** at any time. Your request then shows a reference number (e.g. `TRQ-2026-0007`), and you can follow it on the progress tracker.

**If it's returned to you,** you'll get an email. Open the request, read the remark, click **Amend & resubmit**, make your changes and resubmit. It starts again at your HOD, and earlier versions are kept under **Versions**.

**Other actions:**

- **Clone** a past request to reuse its details.
- **Cancel** a request you no longer need. A reason is required.
- **Print PDF** gives you the official form layout.

## Approvers (HOD, L&D, HR Head)

- You'll get an email or Teams message with an **Open request** button. **Approve or reject only inside the app.**
- **Approvals** in the sidebar lists everything waiting for you, oldest first.
- **Approve:** add an optional remark and confirm. The next stage is notified automatically.
- **Reject & send back:** a remark is required.
  - **HOD:** sends the request back to the requester.
  - **L&D:** choose to send it back to the **Requester** or the **Department HOD**.
  - **HR Head:** choose the **Requester**, the **Department HOD** or the **L&D Team**.
  - Tick who else should be told. The Business Unit Admin is always informed.
- **L&D:** while reviewing, fill in **HRD Corp claimable** and the **grant no.** under *L&D assessment*.
- If someone else acted first, you'll see "already actioned by …" and the page refreshes.

## Business Unit Admins (Land, E&C, Malls)

You're notified when a HOD in your BU approves a request, on every rejection, on final approval and on cancellation. You can view all requests in your BU under **Training RQ history**, plus **Reports** for your BU. No action is required from you.

## HR / L&D: history and reports

- **Training RQ history:** search by ref no., title, requester, provider or **candidate name**. Searching a staff name shows that employee's training history. Filter by status, BU, department, type, HRD Corp and dates, then click **Export to Excel**.
- **Reports:** requests and approved spend by month, business unit and department; planned vs ad hoc; and the average time requests spend at each approval stage. You can export these too.

## System Admins

**Admin settings** lets you manage:

- Business units and BU Admins
- Departments and their HODs
- Roles (L&D Team, HR Head, System Admin)
- Users: fix departments, deactivate leavers, pre-register new HODs
- Notification log, with **Retry** for failed sends
- Audit log of every settings change

Changes apply immediately. For example, a newly assigned HOD sees pending requests from their department straight away.
